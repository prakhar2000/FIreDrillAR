## Intent

Polished pitch/submission video for FireDrill AR (hackathon, Oct 17 2026). Story: problem (drills are boring and
unrealistic) → scan your real room → fire starts by pinch or "fire", spreads, smoke drops, EXIT signs and route
arrows light up → Claude voice coach talks you out → DataFeel wrist haptics (warm on the fire side, buzz in smoke,
cool at the exit) → review screen → team and tech. Polished even where visuals are not perfectly accurate.
